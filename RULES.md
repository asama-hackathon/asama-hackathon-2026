# Rules

## Eligibility
Open to all students of UWC schools and colleges.

## Use of AI tools
Use of AI tools is permitted as a support, but the core logic,
architecture, and understanding of the project must be the team's
own work. Teams may be asked to explain specific parts of their
code during evaluation.

## Academic integrity
- Track A: submissions are monitored by the platform for
  suspicious activity and identical solutions.
- Track B: the full commit history is reviewed, and the demo video must include a team member explaining a specific part of the code.

## Track B demo video
Maximum 3 minutes. It must show the working project AND include
a moment where a team member opens the code and explains one
specific part.

## Evaluation (Track B, 100 points)
- Relevance to the theme: 25
- Technical quality of the code: 25
- Originality and creativity: 20
- Clarity of the demo video: 20
- Completeness of the README: 10

## Decisions
Final results are validated by the Faculty Advisor before
publication.
